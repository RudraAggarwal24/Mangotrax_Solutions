Education & EdTech

Education & EdTech Digital Transformation

Challenge

An education technology organisation experienced:

  • High inquiry volume with low enrollment quality
  • Limited visibility into learner intent and engagement
  • Manual follow-ups and fragmented digital systems

Solution Delivered

Mangotrax implemented:

  • Insight-led digital marketing to identify high-intent learners
  • Conversion-focused websites and inquiry workflows
  • Engagement tracking across landing pages and learning platforms

Data-driven analysis was used to identify inquiry patterns, optimise campaigns, and improve follow-up prioritisation.

KPIs Improved

Enrollment quality
Learner engagement
Inquiry-to-enrollment conversion
